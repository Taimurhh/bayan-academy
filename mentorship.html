How a session works

An hour, one clear next step

Every session ends with one goal for the week, and a written plan for you.

  1. Before

    You book and pick a time. Before the first session, Taimur has a short call with you to hear what’s going on.

  2. The session

    Five minutes with you and your child together on goals. Then a one-to-one conversation, and five minutes together at the end.

  3. After

    You get a written plan: what we talked about, the goal for the week, and how to help at home.

The Mehran Framework

Five stages, from the inside out

Sessions follow the framework of Mehran Education System. Each stage builds on the last.

  1. CoreFaith, values and the daily habits everything else rests on.
  2. CharacterHonesty, adab and self-control, practised, not lectured.
  3. CompetenceFocus, study skills and finishing what you start.
  4. LeadershipTaking charge of a team, a task, a younger sibling.
  5. ContributionPutting it to work for your family and community.

Questions

About Bayan Mentorship

My child doesn’t want to come. Will it still work?

Often, yes. The first session is mostly listening. Many teenagers open up once they see it isn’t another lecture.

Can I book a session just for me, as a parent?

Yes. Choose Bayan Mentorship on the booking form and write “parent session” in the note. We talk about what’s happening at home and agree one thing to try this week.

What do you talk about?

Whatever matters most to your child and to you: habits, school, friends, phones, confidence, faith and purpose. The Mehran Framework gives each conversation a direction.

Can I stay in the session?

You join the first and last five minutes. A parent is always at home during the session. If you’d like to stay for all of it, you can.

Is what my child says private?

Yes, within limits. You hear the goals and the next steps, not every word. If there is any concern about your child’s safety, we tell you straight away.

How many sessions will my child need?

Most families start with four weekly sessions, then decide together whether to continue monthly.

Do sessions continue in Ramadan?

Yes, at times that finish well before iftar.

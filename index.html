How ilm works here

Every class ends with something real

Bayān means clear explanation. So in every programme, you don’t just learn it: you use it, then you explain it. If you can explain it, you know it.

  1. Learn it

    One clear idea per class, taught live by a teacher who checks you’ve got it.

  2. Build it

    Use it straight away: a program, an experiment, a speech, a solved paper.

  3. Explain it

    Show the class what you made and how it works. That’s when it sticks.

Questions

Before you enrol

Which programme is right for my child?

For confident English, Bayan Speak. For future skills, Bayan AI. Bayan Code, STEM and Academics are coming soon. For character and purpose, book a separate one-to-one Bayan Mentorship session. When we call, we’ll help you choose.

Are classes live or recorded?

Always live, with a teacher. Students speak, build and take part; they don’t watch videos.

Is Bayan AI just about using ChatGPT?

No. Students learn how AI works, use it honestly, build small projects with it, and learn when not to rely on it.

Does my child need a laptop?

For Bayan Code and Bayan AI, a laptop or desktop works best. Other programmes work on a phone or tablet with a camera.

How much does it cost?

Fees are monthly and depend on the programme. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll send the current fees.

Can students outside Pakistan join?

Yes. Tell us your time zone and we’ll find a class time that suits you.

Questions

About Bayan Speak

I’m shy. Is this for me?

Yes. Small batches make it easier to start. You speak a little in every class before you speak a lot.

Is this exam tuition?

No. Bayan Speak is about speaking clearly and confidently. You learn the language you need for that, not exam drills.

How do I know which level to join?

When we call you after the form, we’ll check the level together.

What do I need?

A phone, tablet or laptop with a camera and microphone, a steady connection, and a quiet corner.

What happens in Ramadan?

Classes carry on, earlier in the day so they finish well before iftar. There are no classes in Eid week. We confirm the exact schedule with you on WhatsApp.

What if it isn’t right for my child?

Tell us within the first week and we refund that month in full. And if, by week 12, you can’t hear a clear difference in your child’s recordings, the next term is free.

Ready for your turn?

Send the form and we’ll call you to confirm your level and class times before you pay anything.

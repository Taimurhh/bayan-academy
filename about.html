About Bayan Academy

Bayān means clear speech.

Bayān (بیان) is the Arabic and Urdu word for clear explanation: saying what you mean so others understand. That’s our test for knowledge. If you can use it and explain it, you’ve really learned it. We call that ilm that works.

علم جو کام آئے۔

عَلَّمَهُ الْبَيَانَ

“He taught him clear expression.” Qur’an 55:4

In Sūrat al-Raḥmān, bayān is named among the first gifts God gives the human being. The name reminds us what the academy is for.

Questions

About Bayan AI

Is this just about using ChatGPT?

No. Students learn how AI works, use several tools, build small projects, and learn when not to rely on AI at all.

Won’t my child use AI to cheat?

We teach the opposite: honest use is part of every class. Students learn to use AI to understand work, not to hand it in.

Which tools do you use?

Free, age-appropriate tools that run in a browser, chosen per track.

Does my child need a laptop?

A laptop or desktop works best for Bayan AI. A tablet can manage the Explorers track.
